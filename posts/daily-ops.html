Everyday operational tasks

The daily routine keeps backup, messaging, web services and scheduled jobs visible and healthy.

What the checklist covers

  • Confirm the Hermes backup pushed to the private GitHub repository.
  • Confirm the Hermes gateway is active.
  • Check Cloudflare Workers, DNS routes and published services.
  • Verify the main website and blog respond through the edge.
  • Spot-check container health and tunnel connections.
  • Review cron output for failed jobs.
  • Record follow-ups and open questions.

Use the Daily Tasks page to work through the checklist. Completed items are stored in the browser and can be reset each day.
